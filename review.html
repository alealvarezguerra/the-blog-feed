Q-Coffee: Iced Pumpkin Spiced Caramel Latte (featuring. my friend Tauane)

  • Only slight pumpkin flavor was in the whip cream (VERY LIGHT FLAVOR)
  • Has a normal amount of sweetness (what you think an average caramel latte taste like)
  • It looks exactly how its advertised, whip and all!
  • $9.50 for a PSL in SF, I guess that’s ok... still a bit pricey tho
  • Still looks pretty and is aesthetically pleasing

3.1/5

While the coffee looked super cute and it had a nice touch with the crumbled Oreos on top, it didn’t have that pumpkin flavor you would think of when sipping into a pumpkin spice latte. The whip cream itself had a slight pumpkin flavor, but it didn’t last long. I will say that for a caramel latte it does taste good. This was the only PSL I got that wasn’t just a PSL, it was also a caramel latte. There was also a coconut and a matcha flavor, but I wanted to be as close as possible to an original PSL so that I could give it a fare shot. My friend Tauane pointed out that transporting it would also be a bit of a hassle because of how tall the whip cream comes. You would have to basically eat the whip first and then cover it with the lid when you’re on the go.

Avotoasty: Iced Pumpkin Spiced Latte (featuring. my friends Cami and Sophia)

  • For a PSL that is served in an actual pumpkin, it only has a LIGHT pumpkin flavor
  • Was a pretty sweet coffee, but the spices in it has a weird after taste
  • Even if the toppings were so simple, the whip cream was so thick it was just out of this world
  • WAY TOO EXPENSIVE FOR WHAT?!?!
  • Gotta give it to them, the real pumpkin cup is pretty creative

2.8/5

I was really hyping myself up for this one, since I had seen it on my Tik Tok feed for about 2 years now and I thought the idea of it being served in an ACTUAL pumpkin is so cute. Don’t get me wrong, when I finally got it I was super excited to hold it and take photos of it as does everyone else does with it, but the taste overall just disappointed me. It did taste a bit more of a pumpkin flavor than the Q-Coffee, but for a coffee that is getting served in an actual pumpkin, I better taste the actual flavor, not bits of pumpkin guts through my straw. I also CANNOT justify the price on this one. $23??? LIKE WHAT??? Listen what is cute? Yes. But did it taste like a PSL and was the price worth it? HECK NO. There’s definitely better out there

Andytown: Iced Pumpkin Spiced Latte (featuring. just me & Cami)

  • This pumpkin can lowkey give Starbucks a run for its money
  • Its the perfect amount of normal to sweetness
  • Toppings were nice, turns out you can drink through the cinnamon stick
  • The fact that it was $7 should already convince you
  • It’s a latte in a cup, honestly, the simpler the better. Can’t fix what ain’t broke

4.7/5

Hands down... this was the best PSL I have tried in a minute. It’s obviously nothing like a Starbucks PSL, but this coffee was so smooth and creamy, I understood why people were trying to wait for this in line. The flavor had a perfect balance between pumpkin and spice where it didn’t feel overpowering. What really stole the show for me though was their homemade whip cream, or should I say whip butter (lol). Their homemade whip cream was one of the best toppings they could make. It was just the perfect amount of saltiness to sweet ratio. The toppings were so simple, but they went well together. And the best part, IT WAS ONLY 7 DOLLARS!!! IN THIS ECONOMY THAT’S A STEAL. Anyways, Andytown was the very best overall and I will be going back again and again
